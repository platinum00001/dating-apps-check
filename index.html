Honest by design

What this tool will never do

Dating apps don't show outsiders whether someone has a profile or when they were last active. Any site that claims to "scan" a phone number or email is guessing — or worse.

  • ✕
    Look anyone up
    No phone, email, or name searches of other people.
  • ✕
    Scan or scrape apps
    We never contact Tinder, Bumble, or any app for you.
  • ✕
    Store your data
    There is no server or database. It lives in your browser.
  • ✕
    Share without consent
    Nothing is shared unless you copy or send it yourself.

FAQ

Questions

Can this show which apps someone else is on?

No. Apps don't make that information public, and checking someone without their consent isn't okay. Mutual mode only works when both people choose to share their own self-reported status.

Where is my information saved?

In your browser's local storage on this device. Clearing site data, using private browsing, or switching devices will remove it. Use "Clear saved data" on the self-check page to wipe it anytime.

How accurate is a comparison?

It's exactly as accurate as what each person entered. It's a conversation tool built on trust, not proof.

I think someone made a profile pretending to be me.

Use the app's official Help link on the self-check page to report impersonation. Most apps have a dedicated report form and will ask for proof of identity.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's a free static site with no ads, accounts, or tracking.

🔒 Your data stays in this browser. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.
